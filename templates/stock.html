Stock

Reading the chart

Top row
The ticker symbol, up to four letters.
Bottom row
Current price in whole dollars.
White line
The baseline: what the stock cost 32 trading days ago. Everything else is measured against this.
Green
Above the baseline — worth more than it was 32 trading days ago.
Red
Below the baseline — worth less than it was 32 trading days ago.
Bright and dim
The bright line is the closing price for each day. The dimmer band behind it just fills the gap back to the baseline, to make the shape readable at a distance.
Columns
One per trading day, 32 in all. Oldest on the left, today on the right. Weekends and market holidays are skipped, so this covers roughly six and a half calendar weeks.
